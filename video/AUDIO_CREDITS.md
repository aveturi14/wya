## Fonts (not audio, listed here for one place to check)
Geist, Geist Mono, Instrument Serif and Archivo Expanded are all SIL Open Font License 1.1. The licence texts are in `public/fonts/`.

## If you swap the music
Use only a track you have the rights to (for example a royalty-free library track with its licence saved here). Replace `public/audio/music.wav`, keep it at least 1:20 long, and note its title, author, source URL and licence in this file. Cuts are timed to 128.57 BPM, so a track near that tempo will fit best.
