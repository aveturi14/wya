wya restyle

What's holding the current Soft pop / After dark look back, what to fix whatever we choose, and four directions for a sleeker, more premium wya. Each direction has light and dark palettes mapped to the role names in constants/theme.ts, so picking one is a theme swap plus a few kit changes, not a rebuild.

Part 1

What's wrong today

Reviewed in the running app at phone width (light and dark) and in the kit source. File references point at the code to change.

Why it doesn't read as premium yet

  • Everything is equally loud. Cards, buttons, inputs, chips, the bell and the tab bar all get the same 2px outline and hard offset shadow, so nothing leads. Premium interfaces save emphasis for one thing per screen.
  • Saturated fills everywhere. Full-strength lime, violet, pink, green, amber and red blocks compete. The Going / Maybe / Not going tiles on the event page are the loudest thing on it, even at 0.
  • Toy-like details. Tilted stickers, an 800-weight display face at −2 tracking, and generated covers that are one giant letter. They're fun, but they read as a prototype.
  • No quiet tier. Muted text fails contrast, so the only readable greys are fairly dark, and the layout has no soft secondary layer to rest on.

Accessibility

Critical
Muted text fails contrast in both themes
textMuted is 2.6:1 on cream and 3.1:1 on black (4.5:1 needed). It's used for inactive tab labels, placeholders, counts and the Upcoming chevron.
Darken to about 4.6:1 (every palette below already passes) and keep truly decorative greys separate.
constants/theme.ts:50, :74 · components/nav/AppTabBar.tsx:77
Critical
White on violet and white on tangerine fail
The light primary button is 4.1:1, and secondary / onSecondary (the Invited sticker, badges) is 2.9:1. Both are 11–15px text.
Deepen the fills or switch to dark text on them.
constants/theme.ts:54–57
Critical
Buttons aren't announced as buttons
PressableScale sets no accessibilityRole or disabled state. On the sign-in page, "Sign in" shows up as a generic element, and screen readers say nothing about it being tappable or disabled.
Default accessibilityRole="button" and pass accessibilityState={{ disabled }}. Tab bar items get tab plus a selected state.
components/ui/PressableScale.tsx:23 · Button.tsx:37 · AppTabBar.tsx:73
Critical
Motion ignores Reduce Motion
Nothing reads the reduced-motion setting. Confetti, springy presses and staggered card entrances always run.
Use Reanimated's useReducedMotion() in PressableScale, Confetti and the list entrances.
components/ui/Confetti.tsx · PressableScale.tsx · app/(tabs)/home.tsx:501
High
No keyboard focus ring on web
Pressables have no focus style, and TextField removes the outline. Most guests arrive on web through an invite link, so this matters.
Add a 2px focus ring in the primary colour to PressableScale and TextField on web.

High
Text is too small in places
Tab labels and stickers are 11px, the date chip is 10px, captions are 12px, and ten screens override fontSize by hand.
Set a 12px floor for labels and 13px for captions, and add a micro style instead of one-off overrides.

Medium
The bell is under the minimum tap size
On phones, Home passes size={42} (44pt minimum).
Use 44, or keep the 42 visual and add hitSlop.

The page title is the toggle, and the only hint is a small grey chevron. Most people won't find Past events.

Part 2

Four directions

All four drop the chunky outlines and hard shadows, use one accent family in both themes, pass contrast, and calm the status colours. None use frosted glass or gradient backgrounds, since that's what Glow tried and we rolled it back. Switch each mockup between light and dark.
