For the build

The system behind the screens

Colour

  • Dark: page #0B0B0D, card #151518, chip #1F1F23, text #F5F5F7, grey #8A8A93
  • Light: page #FAFAFA, card #FFFFFF, chip #F0F0F2, text #0B0B0D, grey #6E6E76
  • Main button: text colour on page (white pill in dark, black in light). RSVP buttons take the event colour.
  • Sticker pink #FF2E7A for badges and the wya. dot only

Type

  • Geist for the app: 27 / 20 / 15 / 13 / 11.5, weights 400–700, titles at −0.045em
  • Archivo 900 at 125% width for poster titles only
  • Instrument Serif italic for one short line per poster or greeting
  • Geist Mono for dates, codes and money references

Posters

  • Six event looks, each two colours mixed with grain
  • Title, one serif line, date and place. Titles shrink to fit, never run off the edge
  • Stickers: wya's own ("8 going", "you're in", "waiting", "time TBD") plus up to two the host adds, or none
  • Photos replace the gradient; the title stays on top with a shade
  • The glow is the cover blurred behind the page, about 40–60% in dark, 25–30% in light

Shape and motion

  • Corners: 22 posters, 18 cards, 12 fields, pills round
  • No outlines. Cards sit on the page by colour in dark and a soft shadow in light
  • Motion: glow breathes, stickers wobble, confetti on I'm in. All stop for Reduce Motion
